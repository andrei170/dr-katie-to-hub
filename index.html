Compliance Notes (Dental Education + State Board)

+

You are a credentialed dentist and the President of the IABDM. The Accelerator is an education and mentorship program, not a clinical license, so the build stays carefully on the educator side. Everything we ship for you has these guardrails:

  • Licensed-dentists-only language on landing, FAQ, and footer
  • Explicit "does not change your scope of practice or state board obligations" line
  • No specific patient outcome promises, no biological-dentistry-cures-X claims
  • Mercury, ozone, biocompatibility, nutrition, and oral-systemic content framed as your positioning, your teaching, and your own practice
  • Your real on-record patient case attributed to your ACT Dental interview, with "every patient is their own case" framing
  • Self-reported figures (1,000+ CE hours, "youngest MIABDM," DSD + MIABDM uniqueness, over a decade in wellness dentistry) framed as your own statement
  • No fabricated dentist testimonials. None invented. Swap in only on reply.

This keeps you cleanly in the educator-and-mentor lane your IABDM seat occupies, while still letting you sell the Accelerator and the Method with confidence.
